# Manglish Themes in Gaming

Themes of the mangle to be found in gaming.

## Agency

- _game_: important feature (contra vanilla system analysis) is the ontological role of the player: motivations and goals

- _mangle_: essential feature is the (free-)actions of human and non-human actors 

## Resistance

- _game_: participants are frustrated by other players or limitations of agency within the game, i.e. rules 

- _mangle_: (non-)human agency provides pushback against human attempts to problem solve, resolve crises or end controversies

## Contingency

- _game_: outcome may have been different if agents had made different choices

- _mangle_: the conceptual framework that survives is the result of a particular historical path which is not determined by actors or events - choices may always have been different, manifestation of resistance may have been otherwise
